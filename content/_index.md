Aaron Ky-Riesenbach is an infrastructure engineer and musician, currently located in the Boston area, hailing from Los Angeles. He studied computer engineering at Northeastern University.

In his professional life, Aaron currently works at Wolters Kluwer Health, utilizing deep expertise in large-scale systems to develop the health platform. His work focuses on global-scale Kubernetes deployments, utilizing infrastructure as code tools and GitOps practices to build high-quality, reproducible deployment tooling.

Outside of work, Aaron is a passionate musician with interest in a wide variety of musical genres. His primary focus is bluegrass guitar, learning and building on deep musical traditions with American roots.
He is also a lover of jam bands and psychedelic rock, performing and attending Grateful Dead and related music regularly, and has trained as a classical violinist from age 5, regularly winning awards in competition and performing at renowned venues like Carnegie Hall.

Above all else, Aaron lives his life with the goal of bringing joy to those around him.
