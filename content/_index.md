Aaron Ky-Riesenbach is an infrastructure engineer and musician, currently located in the Boston area, hailing from Los Angeles. He studied computer engineering at Northeastern University.

In his professional life, Aaron is an infrastructure engineer, currently at Wolters Kluwer, with deep expertise in large-scale systems. His work focuses on global-scale Kubernetes deployments, utilizing infrastructure as code tools and GitOps practices to build high-quality, reproducible deployment tooling

Outside of work, Aaron is a passionate musician with interest in a wide variety of musical genres. His primary focus is bluegrass guitar, learning and building on deep musical traditions with American roots.
He is also a lover of jam bands and psychedelic rock, performing and attending Grateful Dead and related music regularly.
Prior to his life as a guitarist, Aaron trained as a classical violinist for 18 years, regularly winning awards in competition and performing at renowned venues like Carnegie Hall.

Above all else, Aaron lives his life with the goal of bringing joy to those around him.
