Aaron is a Product Software Engineer in Cloud SRE/DevOps at Wolters Kluwer Health, where he designs and builds platforms serving healthcare software to millions of care providers worldwide.

Much of his current work revolves around Wolters Kluwers's internal AI platform, where he creates solutions to bring agents to every division of Wolters Kluwer, utilizing multi-cloud architecture and robust deployment tooling to guarantee uptime.

As part of the Health division specifically, Aaron develops and maintains a global platform of 50+ Kubernetes clusters serving products such as UpToDate and Lexicomp Online. He utilizes reproducible IaC systems, custom Helm charts and Kubernetes operators, and other modern technical solutions to create automatable, secure systems.

Aaron has a deep interest in distributed systems and enjoys building them at home as well, where he regularly experiments with a Kubernetes/Proxmox-based home lab to learn about the latest and greatest in cloud technologies.
